PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026

Critical Alert Lane is designed to work without an account. Your reminder titles, notes, schedules, settings, and acknowledgement history are stored in IndexedDB on your device. We do not receive or read them.

Sample demo

The sample demo uses a separate local database named demo:critical-alert-lane. It does not read or write your real reminder database, license token, or Android scheduler. Leaving the demo discards its sample data.

Permissions

The app asks for notification permission only when you choose “Enable notifications.” It does not request contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, or location access.

Exports

When you export a JSON file, your browser saves an unencrypted copy containing your reminders and history. You control where that file goes. Imports are read locally and replace device data only after confirmation.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlimited unlock, checkout and license verification are provided by Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record. This app stores the returned license token and a daily verification result in local storage. Payment details are not entered into or stored by this app.

Network use

The installed app caches its shell for offline use. It contacts the Sociobot billing API only for checkout or license verification. There are no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party font requests.

Delete your data

Delete reminders in the app, clear this site’s browser storage, or uninstall the app. Removing a license from Settings deletes its local token but does not cancel or refund the purchase.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
