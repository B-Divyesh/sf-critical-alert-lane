Privacy

Effective August 29, 2026.

Critical Alert Lane works without an account.

Reminder titles, notes, schedules, settings, and acknowledgement history stay in this browser on your device.

We do not receive or read reminder data.

Sample demo

The sample demo uses the separate demo:critical-alert-lane database.

It never reads or writes your real reminders, license token, or Android scheduler.

Leaving the demo discards its changes.

Permissions

The app asks for notification access only after you choose “Enable notifications” in settings.

It does not request contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, or location access.

Android may offer exact-alarm access from settings.

Android uses a battery-aware inexact alarm when you decline that access.

Exports

JSON exports contain your reminders and history without encryption.

You choose where each export goes.

Imports stay local and replace device data only after confirmation.

Purchases

Sociobot provides checkout and license checks.

Dodo processes the payment and handles refunds through Sociobot checkout.

The app stores your license token and one daily check result in local storage.

You never enter payment details into this app. This app does not store them.

Network use

The installed app caches its shell for offline use.

Only checkout and license checks contact the Sociobot billing API.

The app loads no ads, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts.

Delete your data

Delete reminders, clear this site’s storage, or uninstall the app.

Removing a local license does not cancel or refund its purchase.

Contact

Email privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.

Repeat critical Android reminders until you snooze or acknowledge them.
